Pizza

Description

Pizza is a world-famous Italian dish. A classic pizza combines a crust that is crispy on the outside and tender on the inside with melted cheese and vibrant tomato sauce.

Ingredients

  • Dough: 500g bread flour (preferably Tipo 00), 7g active dry yeast, 325ml warm water, 10g salt, and 1 tablespoon olive oil.
  • Sauce: 1 can (400g) crushed tomatoes (preferably San Marzano), 1 minced garlic clove, 1 teaspoon dried oregano, and salt to taste.
  • Topping: 250g fresh mozzarella cheese (shredded or sliced) and fresh basil leaves

Steps

  1. Dough: Mix flour, yeast, and salt. Add water and oil. Knead for 10 minutes until smooth
  2. Rise: Let the dough rest in a greased, covered bowl for 2 hours or until doubled in size
  3. Sauce: Mix the crushed tomatoes with garlic, oregano, and salt in a bowl. No cooking required.
  4. Shape: Divide dough into two balls. Stretch each portion with your hands from the center outward, leaving the edges thicker.
  5. Assemble: Spread a thin layer of sauce over the dough. Distribute the mozzarella cheese evenly.
  6. Bake: Preheat your oven to its absolute maximum temperature (500°F / 250°C or higher). Bake on a hot tray or pizza stone for 8 to 12 minutes until the crust is golden and cheese bubbles.
  7. Finish: Remove from the oven and top with fresh basil leaves before slicing.
