Completo Italiano

Description

The completo italiano features a boiled vienesa (hot dog), fresh chopped tomato, hand-mashed avocado, and mayonnaise, all assembled on a copihue roll.

The red, green, and white colors mimic the Italian flag—hence the name. It is Chile's most popular sandwich and is ready in 20 minutes.

Ingredients

  • 4 hot dog buns (specifically slightly sweet, soft Chilean-style completo rolls if available)
  • 4 hot dog sausages or Vienna-style vienesas
  • 2 ripe medium avocados (palta)
  • 2 ripe tomatoes
  • Mayonnaise (generous amount)
  • Salt and a few drops of oil (for seasoning)
  • Optional: ketchup, mustard, or ají (hot sauce)

Steps

  1. Cook the sausages: Boil the vienesas in hot water for 5 minutes (or lightly pan-sear them in a skillet with a tiny bit of oil for extra flavor).
  2. Prepare the tomato: Peel the tomatoes if desired, then dice them into small cubes. Season with a pinch of salt and a few drops of oil
  3. Prepare the avocado: Peel the avocados and mash them with a fork into a thick, creamy pure. Season with a little salt to taste
  4. Warm the bread: Slice the hot dog buns down the top (lengthwise) and warm them in an oven for 2 to 3 minutes or lightly toast them so they stay soft and warm
  5. Assemble: Place a hot sausage inside each warm bun. Add a layer of diced tomatoes, generously cover with the mashed avocado, and crown the top with a thick layer of mayonnaise. Serve immediately.
