French fries

Description

French fries are long, thin pieces of potato that are deep-fried until crisp on the outside and soft inside.

The main components are russet potatoes, neutral cooking oil, and salt. The cooking process uses a double-fry method for optimal texture.

Ingredients

  • Russet potatoes: 4 large potatoes (about 2 pounds / 900 g), peeled
  • Neutral oil: 2 quarts (1.9 L) of peanut or vegetable oil for frying
  • Salt: 1/4 cup kosher salt (or regular salt to taste)
  • Water: Cold water for soaking

Steps

  1. Cut: Slice peeled potatoes into uniform 1/4-inch thick batons.
  2. Soak: Submerge the cut potatoes in cold water for 30 minutes to remove excess starch
  3. Dry: Drain the potatoes completely and pat them thoroughly dry with paper towels
  4. First Fry (Blanch): Heat oil to 300°F (150°C). Cook the potatoes in small batches for 4 to 5 minutes until soft and pale, but not browned
  5. Cool: Remove the fries and let them cool completely on a wire rack.
  6. Second Fry (Crisp): Heat the oil to 375°F (190°C). Fry the cooled potatoes again for 1 to 2 minutes until golden brown and crispy.
  7. Season: Transfer the hot fries to a bowl lined with paper towels and toss with salt immediately
